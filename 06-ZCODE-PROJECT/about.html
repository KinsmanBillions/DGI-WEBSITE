Our story

Print media and branding, done with pride.

De-Greens Innovation is a print media and branding firm dedicated to providing top-notch printing and branding solutions to businesses and organizations. With experienced professionals and state-of-the-art equipment, we deliver high-quality print and branding products that exceed our clients' expectations.

In a world where growth is paramount, we see ourselves as the nurturing soil that cultivates ideas into vibrant blooms of success. With each print, we don't just spread messages — we plant seeds of possibility that blossom into thriving campaigns.

From start-ups and schools to restaurants, retail stores and non-profits, we help brands make a lasting impression.

Our identity

A mark grown from meaning.

Our logo-mark was derived from our name and our mission — De-Greens, media, growth. The name of the brand carries leaves within the letterforms, and the “E” stretches outward as a symbol of longevity.

  • The D + leaf — growth rooted in identity
  • The O + leaf — ideas coming to full fruition
  • The stretched E — longevity, lasting impression and strength

Two greens carry the brand everywhere it goes: Royal Green #0D5B11 and Verse Green #187C19 — the colours of renewal you'll find across everything we print, including this site.
