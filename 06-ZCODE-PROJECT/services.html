Services

Print, branding and advertising — under one roof.

Digital and offset printing, identity design, campaign print and packaging, produced in our own workshop in Nungua Barrier, Accra. One team, one standard of quality.

01 · Digital printing

Everyday business print, done properly.

Business cards, flyers, brochures and posters — the materials your business runs on, produced in-house on our digital presses. Short runs stay affordable and deadlines stay achievable.

Send us print-ready artwork, or let our team prepare and check your files. Either way, every job passes our prepress checklist before a single sheet is printed.

  • Short and mid-size runs, without minimum-order pressure
  • Consistent, accurate colour from proof to final piece
  • Quick turnarounds for time-sensitive jobs

02 · Offset printing

Consistent colour, thousands of times over.

When quantities grow, offset printing delivers sharp, uniform colour across long runs — at a lower cost per unit. It's the economical choice for bulk communication.

Jobs are folded, trimmed, counted and wrapped in our workshop, so your print arrives organised and ready to distribute.

  • Cost-effective at high volumes
  • Sharp, consistent colour across every copy
  • Finishing and packing handled in-house
